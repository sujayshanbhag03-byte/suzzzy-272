I'm currently a student at PACE PU College in Shivamogga, Karnataka. My interests extend beyond the classroom into business, technology, creative work and understanding how financial markets operate.

I've experimented with different business ideas, from clothing concepts to websites for local businesses. Some attempts didn't work out. Others taught me how to communicate, sell, build and improve.

I'm particularly interested in the process of turning an idea into something useful — identifying a real problem, developing a solution, presenting it well and learning from the response.

Away from business, aviation has a special place in my life. I'm fascinated by aircraft and the possibility of becoming a commercial pilot. I also enjoy badminton, Hindustani classical music, films and exploring new places.
